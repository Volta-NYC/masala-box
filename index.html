Our Story

Named after the
masala dabba.

Masala Box is an essential tool box at the core of all Indian cooking, a round stainless steel container carrying an array of spices. It is to every Indian chef what a palette is to a painter. Traditionally it is referred to as a dabba, or box.

My fondest memories growing up in our humble home were of family members gathered around for dinner, sharing bread and dishes over stories of the day. Our family history of migration took us from India, to Philippines, to Europe, to Canada, and now the US.

I found that despite regional and cultural differences, we are all intrinsically alike. Eating and drinking together is the simplest form of pure joy and affirmation of being a family, as well as connecting with friends and integrating into a community.

It is unfortunate that the conventional understanding when it comes to Indian food is "hot and spicy." A flavorful Indian meal requires understanding of how spices work together. It is a science and an art, based on Ayurvedic principles of well-being. Every spice used in Indian cooking has health benefits that go beyond flavor and nourishment.

The emphasis is always on freshness and flavor; "hotness" is optional. My life's journey has brought me here. We welcome you to our home. We are here to create, here to serve, and above all here to connect.

Khao, pio, aur jiyo
Eat, drink, and live
Sumita Gohal-Verma · Executive Chef
